# Privacy Policy for CKP Festivals Donation App

**Last Updated: March 03, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the CKP Festivals App ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects user information in connection with festival donation management and WhatsApp-based communication services.

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is voluntarily provided by donors or authorized festival volunteers:

Name – To personalize donation receipts and communication.

Phone Number – To send automated WhatsApp donation receipts or event-related messages.

Donation Amount – To include in the donation receipt and maintain accounting records.

Donation Date – For audit and record-keeping purposes.

Email Address (if login is used) – If administrators log in using email authentication, their email address is securely stored for authentication and access control purposes only.

We do not collect unnecessary personal data.

### 2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used strictly for the following purposes:

To generate and send a one-time donation receipt via WhatsApp.

To send approved festival-related notifications (if applicable).

To maintain donation records for festival accounting and audit requirements.

To authenticate administrators who log in using email credentials.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

### 3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the application:

Meta WhatsApp Business API – Used to deliver automated WhatsApp messages. Phone numbers and message content are processed by Meta in accordance with their privacy policy.

Firebase Authentication (if enabled) – Used for secure admin login.

Firebase Realtime Database – Used to store donation and contact records securely.

These services process data according to their respective privacy policies.

### 4. Data Retention

Donation records are retained only for the duration required for festival accounting and audit compliance.

Personal contact details are removed from active messaging logs after the audit period.

Administrator email accounts remain stored only while access to the system is required.

We do not retain personal data longer than necessary.

### 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Access to administrative features is restricted to authorized users only.

### 6. Consent

By providing:

A phone number, the donor consents to receive a one-time automated WhatsApp message related to their donation.

An email address for login, the administrator consents to secure storage of their email for authentication purposes.

Users may request removal of their data by contacting us.
