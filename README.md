# Privacy Policy for CKP Festivals Donation App

**Last Updated: February 07, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the CKP Festivals App ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information, specifically regarding the sending of donation receipts via the WhatsApp Business API.

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect the following information provided voluntarily by donors or festival volunteers:
* **Name:** To personalize the thank-you message.
* **Phone Number:** Specifically to send the donation receipt via WhatsApp.
* **Donation Amount:** To include in the automated receipt.

### 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
* To generate and send a one-time donation receipt via WhatsApp.
* To maintain a record of donations for festival accounting.
We **do not** sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for marketing purposes.

### 3. Third-Party Services
We use the **Meta WhatsApp Business API** to deliver messages. Your data (phone number and message content) is processed by Meta in accordance with their privacy policy.

### 4. Data Retention
We retain donation records only for the duration of the festival audit period, after which personal contact details are deleted from our active messaging logs.

### 5. Consent
By providing a phone number for a donation receipt, the donor consents to receive a one-time automated message via WhatsApp for that specific transaction.
